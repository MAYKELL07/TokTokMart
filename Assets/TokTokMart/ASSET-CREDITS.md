# Current art and audio credits

The active scene uses the supplied TokTokMart environment, Sam/Zack/Toyol drawings and animations, November UI exports and Bigbesty font. The November 11 bundle adds the counter, storage box, task cards and star artwork. The artists retain ownership of their supplied work. Kenney resources below provide additional artwork.


The supporting non-pixel assets are downloaded from Kenney. All packs below are CC0 and permit personal, educational and commercial use. Original licenses are saved under ThirdPartyLicenses.

| Racing Pack | Bo's car and light-fixture graphic | https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-pack |

Mini Market's included credits also name Fleur Keijsers and Guus Vermeulen. Kenney is the creator/distributor of the packs. These are temporary casting assets for the named GDD characters, not official character designs.

Adaptations: HD character poses are padded to a consistent canvas and mirrored for left-facing movement. 3D market and blaster models are rendered with an orthographic camera and a palette shader. White splat artwork is tinted blue in Unity. A white barrier graphic is reused as a simple fluorescent fixture. No generative image tool was used.

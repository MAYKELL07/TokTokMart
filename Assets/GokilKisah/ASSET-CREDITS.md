# Current art and audio credits

The active shop now uses free non-pixel assets downloaded from Kenney. All packs below are CC0 and permit personal, educational and commercial use. Original licenses are saved under ThirdPartyLicenses; downloaded archives are retained under Downloads at the project root.

| Pack | Used for | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Toon Characters | Sam, Zack, shoppers and encounter stand-ins; supplied HD idle, back, side and walk poses | https://kenney.nl/assets/toon-characters |
| Mini Market 1.0 | Shelving, checkout, baskets, stockroom displays and walls; original FBX models baked into 512px sprites | https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-market |
| Prototype Textures | Seamless shop, storage and street floor grids | https://kenney.nl/assets/prototype-textures |
| Game Icons | Phone, power switch and counter-can markers | https://kenney.nl/assets/game-icons |
| Splat Pack | Blue-tinted water spill effect | https://kenney.nl/assets/splat-pack |
| Blaster Kit 2.1 | Non-pixel weapon stand-in for the nail-gun encounter | https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit |
| Racing Pack | Bo's car and light-fixture graphic | https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-pack |

Mini Market's included credits also name Fleur Keijsers and Guus Vermeulen. Kenney is the creator/distributor of the packs. These are temporary casting assets for the named GDD characters, not official character designs.

Adaptations: HD character poses are padded to a consistent canvas and mirrored for left-facing movement. 3D market and blaster models are rendered with an orthographic camera and a palette shader. White splat artwork is tinted blue in Unity. A white barrier graphic is reused as a simple fluorescent fixture. No generative image tool was used. See ThirdParty/KenneyToonCharacters/sprite-sources.json and Verification/art-source-audit.txt for exact mappings.

Current runtime resources are in Resources/OnlineArt. Previous pixel sheets and the prior custom vector artwork remain as legacy references; they are superseded in the playable scene.
